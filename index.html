Academic Profile

Dr. Mohd. Yaseen Khan is a distinguished academician with over 20.5 years of experience, blending 18 years of teaching excellence with 2.5 years of industry insight.

Currently serving as an Associate Professor at Vivekananda Global University, Jaipur, he is an expert in Marketing, International Business, and General Management. Dr. Khan holds a Ph.D. from Uttarakhand Technical University and has been honored with the prestigious Rashtrapati Puruskar by the President of India for his social service contributions.
