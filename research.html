Retail Management

The retail service quality scale study: examining generalisability and applicability

Journal: Int. J. Business and Systems Research (Feb 6, 2025)

Abstract: This study assesses the generalisability of the retail service quality scale (RSQS) in multiple specialty stores in a tier II city in Northern India. Findings demonstrate that all dimensions equally contribute to customers’ perceptions of service quality.

Bibliometric Analysis

Retrospection of online reviews research landscape

Journal: Int. J. Process Management and Benchmarking (Sep 17, 2024)

Abstract: A bibliometric analysis of 1,469 documents from Scopus to amalgamate publication patterns of online reviews. Identifies clusters like e-word-of-mouth, consumer reviews, and social media analytics.

Strategic Management

Strategic Management of Technological Disruption

Journal: European Economic Letters (EEL) (Aug 1, 2024)

Abstract: Explores how companies can navigate the impact of emerging technologies like AI and blockchain. Suggests proactive adaptation to technological changes for long-term success.

Assessing the Viability of Blockchain Technology for Supply Chain Management

Journal: CAHIERS MAGELLANES-NS (Jul 10, 2024)
