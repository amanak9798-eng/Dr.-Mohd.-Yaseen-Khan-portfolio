Academic Narrative

Dr. Mohd. Yaseen Khan is an accomplished academician with expertise in Marketing, International Business & General Management. With over 20.5 years of rich experience (18 years in teaching and 2.5 years in industry), he has dedicated his career to shaping the minds of future leaders.

He is currently supervising two Ph.D. scholars at Noida International University (NIU) and has previously supervised scholars from Bangladesh and Afghanistan. He serves as an External Examiner for Ph.D. at Mandsaur University, MP. During his tenure at the Army Institute, he contributed to the MDP for UP Judicial Officers in 2011.
